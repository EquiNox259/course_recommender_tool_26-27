About the DAV Team

We are the Data Analytics and Visualization (DAV) Team, a part of the Undergraduate Academic Council (UGAC) at IIT Bombay. Founded in 2018, the DAV Team is an interdisciplinary group dedicated to analysing institutional data to provide valuable insights for students and actionable solutions for the administration. Our core mission is to harness the power of data analytics to inform policy development, while offering students hands-on experience with real-world data and enhancing their expertise in data science.
